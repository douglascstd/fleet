This module is an add-on for the Fleet application in Odoo. It adds a
register of the SIM cards/chips used by the fleet, with their contract
information:

- Phone number, IMEI and carrier of each SIM card.
- Contract information: contract and expiration dates, billing period
  (monthly or annual) and contract amount in the company currency.
- Phone number and IMEI are unique per company.
- Access rights based on the Fleet groups, plus a dedicated SIM
  administrator group to manage the register.

Usage
=====

To manage the SIM cards of the fleet, go to *Fleet > Configuration > SIM
Cards > SIM Cards* and create one record per chip, filling the phone
number, the carrier and the contract information.
